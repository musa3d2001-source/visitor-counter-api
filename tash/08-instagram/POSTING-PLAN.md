- [ ] Move the files to the phone **as originals**: AirDrop, a cable, or a Drive/Files download. **Not WhatsApp**, which recompresses video and PNGs.
- [ ] Name, bio, link, category, contact buttons and highlights done (§2). The WhatsApp link tested from another phone.
- [ ] The phone number placeholder `965XXXXXXXX` is replaced everywhere it appears (bio link, any DM template you use).

**For every post**
- [ ] Upload the files listed in its `caption.md`. Carousels go in **slide order 01 → last**; check the order in the preview before posting.
- [ ] **Reels:** choose `cover.png` from the camera roll as the cover. Leave the profile-grid crop **centred**: the covers are designed for the centre 3:4. "Also share to feed" stays ON.
- [ ] **4:5 posts:** keep 4:5 (don't let the app crop to 1:1).
- [ ] **Audio:** the reels have our own original audio. Add no music from the app library, and don't let the app swap the sound.
- [ ] **Caption:** paste the `text` block from `caption.md` exactly. In the preview, check that «؟» and «!» sit at the **left** end of Arabic lines and the line breaks survived.
- [ ] **Hashtags:** at most 8, already in the caption. If the app refuses eight, keep the first five.
- [ ] **Alt text:** Advanced settings → accessibility → write alt text (Arabic, from `caption.md`, one per carousel slide). If the app doesn't offer it for reels, skip it.
- [ ] **AI label: switch it ON for posts 1, 2 and 9, and leave it OFF for the code-made posts.** Do it **before you tap Share**, on the last screen of the composer: open the advanced options and turn on the AI label toggle (in the English app it has been called "Add AI label"; the published post then shows "AI info" by the account name).
