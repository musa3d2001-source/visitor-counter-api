That stopped being true when the launch ad took the photoreal burger and coffee (3.50–6.00): those two product shots are AI footage too, and the jewellery ring was never from a concept ad (it is a 3D build made for this film). The line now reads «المنتجات بالإعلان كلها خيالية، والأكل ومشاهد الناس سويناها بالذكاء الاصطناعي.», which is true shot by shot (`04-launch-ad/SCRIPT.md` §2 and §5):
