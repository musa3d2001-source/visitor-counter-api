## 4. Video gates

**Opener (Seedance 2.5). Order: approved still, one 480p draft (12 credits), gate, then the 1080p final (64 credits). The price is confirmed to the owner before each step.**
The 480p draft passes only if all of these hold (times in seconds, so they hold at any native frame rate):
1. Frame 0 of the draft matches the approved still (same layout; SSIM 0.90 or more at draft size).
2. Native frame rate and frame count are read with `ffprobe` and recorded; this picks column A or B of the remap table.
3. The ice hits the surface inside the first 0.30 s.
4. The splash crown is clearly above the rim by 0.45 s and nothing rises above y 880 (scaled) at any time.
5. The surface is calm by 2.0 s and stays calm to the end (the CTA band uses the last 1.6 s).
6. The camera does not move; no zoom, no drift, no cut.
7. No new object, hand, straw, text or morphing glass; ice stays ice.
8. No duplicate or frozen frames except at the very end (`mpdecimate` count 2 or fewer).
If the draft fails: one more draft (12, earmarked in the reserve) with a corrected prompt. If that fails too, the owner chooses between a Kling opener (5.25) and the zero-credit Lime opener; no blind 1080p attempt.
After the 1080p final: the same eight checks, plus frame 0 SSIM 0.95 or more against the approved still (else the still itself is drawn at f0). A second 1080p take (64) is never ordered without the owner's OK.

**Kling shots (3 s each, 5.25 credits, sound off).** Each clip passes only if:
- camera static, no cut, no new object, hand or text, nothing enters y 0-880;
- S5: the nuts land inside the first 0.4 s and the syrup sheen is visibly spreading; S2: puff onset p at native frame 10 or earlier, smoke below y 880; S3: bun impact i between native frames 10 and 20, burger stays a burger; S6: the ring lands with a visible splash inside the first 0.3 s;
- 72 native frames present, no duplicate run.
A failed clip gets one retake from the reserve (5.25). S6 failing twice means B-20 is not delivered in this round (owner decides); A-20 is not affected.
