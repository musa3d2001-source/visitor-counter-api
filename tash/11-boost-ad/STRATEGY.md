## 0. Executive answer (the strategy on one page)

1. **One new idea, built in code.** The three typing dots (•••) stop: nobody is talking about your project. Then TASH's own
   dots get up, *make the ad*, and the whole chat starts typing. The brand mark is the story, from the first frame to the loop.
   Working title **«محد يسولف عنه؟»**. It is not a re-cut of the launch ad, and it drops the launch ad's before/after idea and lines (owner, 9 Nov).
2. **Single job.** Make **طشّ** the name a Kuwaiti business owner remembers when they need an ad (the ~95% not buying now),
   and move the ~5% who are buying now to send a DM. Single-minded proposition: **«نسوي الإعلان نفسه.»** The feeling it sells is
   pride in being talked about, not a list of services.
3. **Hook = frames 0–30 do the work.** Frame 0 is already moving: a lime typing bubble, typing ticks on the soundtrack, and the owner
   call-out «مشروعك حلو…» readable by 0.5 s. The dots *die* at 1.4 s («محد يسولف عنه؟»), revive at 2.5 s, and splash into **«طشّ»**
   at 3.0 s, with the proposition readable by about 3.5 s. Brand asset at 0.0 s, brand name by 3.1 s, no logo intro, no face needed.
4. **Three hooks on one identical body (3.00–20.00 s):** H1 «dots that stop» (ranked 1), H2 category roll-call «مطعم؟ كوفي؟ عطور؟»
   (ranked 2, plus a women-coded H2w «عبايات؟ ميكب؟ حلويات؟»), H3 «impossible object», where three lime 3D spheres land (ranked 3).
5. **Length.** A **20.0 s master** for both platforms, a **12 s cutdown** (≤15 s, so ThruPlay = completion on Meta), and an optional
   **6 s bumper**. No 27 s cut: nothing shows that length itself helps, and the peak-end and attention evidence favour 15–20 s.
6. **Structure.** Surprise (0–3) → name + proposition (3–5) → one continuous **craft one-take**, where the dots become studio lights
   and a 3D product builds wire → clay → final (5–8.5) → the ad goes out (8.5–9.75) → **the chat wakes up** in rising bursts
   (9.75–12.75) → **peak = brand**: every dot on screen converges into the ش (12.75–15) → TASH *replies* with the CTA (15–20) →
   the last frame matches frame 0, so the video loops.
7. **CTA is DM only** (owner rule). Instagram: «اطلب إعلانك بالخاص» + @tash.creative.kw, matched to a **"Send message" button →
   Instagram Direct**. TikTok: «دزّ لنا ••• بالخاص» + @tash.creative, where the ••• doubles as an attribution keyword. No WhatsApp, no phone, no price on screen.
8. **Boost setup.** Post organically first, then promote *that* post. Instagram: **Ads Manager → Engagement → Messaging apps →
   Instagram only → maximise conversations**, broad Kuwait 18+, Advantage+ creative enhancements **off**. Week 1 is a **Meta A/B test of
   H1/H2/H3**, judged on hook rate × hold × cost per conversation. From week 2, **one** consolidated ad set. TikTok: a Spark Ad of the organic post;
   use a DM-conversations goal only if it exists for Kuwait, otherwise Profile visits. Fund TikTok only once Meta needs are met (≥ about KWD 12/day in total).
9. **Budget by formula, not benchmarks.** No credible Kuwait CPM or cost-per-conversation data exists. Run a calibration buy until
   10–15 conversations. To leave learning, a Meta ad set needs a daily budget of about **7 × cost per conversation** (Meta's rule of thumb is 10×).
   Break-even cost per conversation ≈ close rate × first order (about KWD 2–4.5 at a 5% close on KWD 40–90). The owner's budget is the missing input.
10. **Honesty is part of the craft.** The paid cut is **100% code-made (LAYL 3D, ring 3D, type, motion, original score)**, so it needs
    **no AI label** on either platform. Concept work stays slated on screen *while it plays*. The chat bubbles are stylised, with no names or numbers.
    No platform UI or fake buttons, no engagement bait, no promise of views or sales. MOR, the concept photoreal pieces and AI people
    go into a separate **labelled** test arm later, never into the launch cut.

## 1. The audience and the single job of this ad

**Who.** Kuwaiti owners of small and medium businesses that sell on Instagram and through DMs: restaurants, cafés («كوفي»),
perfume and oud, abayas and fashion, beauty, retail, and home businesses (sweets, perfume, abayas). Decision makers are mostly Kuwaiti
nationals [W]. They live on Instagram and TikTok (both platforms reach most adults in Kuwait; [DataReportal 2026](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-kuwait)
[M]. Use this as an order of magnitude only, because ad-tool reach overstates real people, see §9). They are B2B buyers, and only ~5% of them are
in the market at any moment ([Dawes / B2B Institute 95:5](https://www.marketingweek.com/peter-weinberg-jon-lombardo-95-5-rule) [M]).

**What they believe now.**
- «طشّ؟ ما سمعت فيهم.» They don't know us. TASH has zero awareness, no clients and no testimonials.
- «إعلان = صورة حلوة بالتلفون.» Or the opposite: «الإعلان الزين يبيله وكالة وميزانية.»
- «منتجي زين… الناس بتعرفه.» The product is good, but nobody talks about it. This is the category entry point we own
  (cta-bank hook ٤: «مشروعك حلو… بس محد يسولف عنه؟»).
- Their blockers: not knowing where to start, no time, doubting it's needed, cost ([Wyzowl 2025/2026](https://wyzowl.com/video-marketing-statistics/), global marketers [M]).

**What we want them to believe, then do.**
- Believe: «طشّ يسوون الإعلان نفسه — فكرة لين ملف نهائي — وبمستوى يخلّي الناس تسولف.» The work on screen is the proof.
- Feel: *pride and possibility*: «مشروعي يستاهل الناس تسولف عنه.» Personal value moves B2B buyers about twice as much as business value
  ([CEB/Google/Motista](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/210152/googleceb.html) [M]). Emotional work builds the long-term memory
  ([Binet & Field, IPA](https://ipa.co.uk/media/14463/long_and_short_of_it_presentation_final_030424.pdf) [M]).
- Do: the ~5% send a DM now. The ~95% remember **طشّ + ••• + the sound** when the need arrives.

**The single job.** *Link one feeling («يسولفون عنك») to one name (طشّ) and one asset (•••), and leave one next step (DM).*
Binet's warning stands: one execution that chases brand *and* hard response "will probably fail at both"
([Marketing Week](https://www.marketingweek.com/can-you-achieve-long-and-short-at-the-same-time-usually-no/) [M]). So the film is
brand-led with one clean activation ending. Activation lives in the *objective* (messaging) and the *button*, not in a
service menu. **Decision:** no price list, no service list, no guarantees on screen. Those go to the caption, the profile and the DM welcome.

## 2. The hook doctrine

### 2.1 What must happen in frames 0–30 (the first second at 30 fps)

| When | Must happen | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **f0** | A finished, moving composition. No black frame, no fade, no logo. The **dots are already the action** (typing), placed off-centre. | Ad gist is grasped in <100 ms ([Pieters & Wedel 2012](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:inm:ormksc:v:31:y:2012:i:1:p:59-73) [S]); mobile recall at 0.25 s, dwell 1.7 s ([Facebook IQ via Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/facebook-why-mobile-video-ads-must-work-fast/446217/) [M]); central brand positions raise avoidance and pulses lower it ([Teixeira, Wedel & Pieters 2010](https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ormksc/v29y2010i5p783-804.html) [S]); logo overlays cut awareness lift, while brand cues built into the action help ([System1 × TikTok](https://chiefmarketer.com/system1-tiktok-study-branding-elements-and-creator-led-content-improve-short-form-video-ads/) [M]) |
| **f0–f9 (≤0.3 s)** | A **motion onset** (the bubble pops in or the dots start) and a **sound transient** tied to it (typing ticks) | Motion *onset* captures attention, continuous drift does not ([Abrams & Christ 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12930472/) [S]); >75% of Reels views are sound-on ([Meta 2024](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2024/11/07/unlock-the-power-of-reel-ads/) [M]) |
| **f6–f15 (0.2–0.5 s)** | ≤3 words at ≥120 px, readable by 0.5 s, that **call out the owner** («مشروعك…») | CRAFT-ADDENDUM §2.1; reading takes ≈0.25 s/word for English and longer for Arabic ([Brysbaert 2019](https://www.Gwern.net/doc/psychology/linguistics/2019-brysbaert.pdf) [S]). The algorithm delivers broadly, so the first words must do the targeting ([TikTok targeting guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/best-practices-for-targeting) [M]) |
| **f15–f30 (0.5–1.0 s)** | One focal point. A question is open in the viewer's head. | Attention dispersion predicts zapping (Teixeira 2010 [S]); curiosity gaps work when they resolve fast ([Loewenstein; Kang 2009](https://neuroecon.berkeley.edu/papers/papers_files/Kang_PsychSci_2009.html) [S]) |
| **by ~1.5 s** | **Surprise**: an expectation is broken | The *level* of surprise concentrates attention ([Teixeira et al. 2012](https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/emotion-induced-engagement-in-internet-video-ads/) [S]); 72% of gaze shifts go to surprising locations ([Itti & Baldi 2009](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2860069) [S]) |
| **by 3.0–3.5 s** | The gap resolves honestly, the **name «طشّ»** is shown, and the **proposition** is readable | TikTok: proposition in the first 3 s, hook within 6 s ([creative best practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices) [M]); early brand recognition is linked to much larger memory and awareness lift (System1 × TikTok, 887 ads [M]); Meta penalises withheld-information hooks ([Jon Loomer on Meta low-quality attributes](https://www.jonloomer.com/low-quality-ad-content-and-post-click-experiences/) [M]) |
| **by 6 s** | The viewer who leaves now still knows *who* (طشّ, •••) and *what* (they make ads). The early CTA is the platform's own **"Send message" button** and the caption, not an extra on-screen slate. | Most measured value arrives in the first seconds (TikTok/MediaScience; FB/Nielsen "up to 47% of ad-recall lift from views ≤3 s" [M]) |

**Never in the hook:** «انتظر للآخر», «ما راح تصدق», «علّق…», a static centred wordmark, a slow push-in from a dark frame, a fake
notification, or a financial-distress line («مبيعاتك طايحة؟»), which Meta treats as a personal attribute.

### 2.2 Three hook archetypes, ranked for TASH

All three end on **the same frame at 3.00 s**: the dots dive and splash. Only 0.00–3.00 s changes, so a hook test measures
the hook and nothing else.

| Rank | Hook | Frames 0–90 (0–3.0 s) | Why this rank |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · H1 «النقاط وقفت» (the dots stop)** | brand-native expectation break | **0.00** ink, grain; a lime typing bubble (TASH's rounded bubble, not any app's) is already typing, with ticks under it. **0.20** «مشروعك حلو…» enters word by word, RTL (Alexandria 800, 130 px), readable by 0.5. **1.35** the line swaps to «محد يسولف عنه؟». Owner call-out in word 1. Brand in frame 0. Pain that is about attention, not money. Code-only. Brand-new: nothing from the launch ad. |
| **2 · H2 «مطعم؟ كوفي؟ عطور؟» (roll-call)** | direct self-selection | **0.00** three small lime typing bubbles stacked RTL, all typing. **0.20 / 0.60 / 1.00** each bubble resolves into one word: «مطعم؟» «كوفي؟» «عطور؟», and the words *stay*, so the line is fully held from 1.0 to 2.5. **2.55** all three bubbles' dots break loose and gather into one row. **3.00** they dive → splash. **H2w** (women-coded, same timing): «عبايات؟ ميكب؟ حلويات؟». | Highest relevance under broad targeting: owners see their category in under a second. | **3 · H3 «إعلانك يبيله سالفة.» (impossible object)** | craft spectacle | **0.00** three lime 3D spheres (Three.js) are already falling onto a glossy ink floor. **0.30** impact on the contact frame (tash_hit + clap), and they bounce in a *typing* rhythm. **0.40** «إعلانك يبيله سالفة.» (3 words). **1.6–2.5** they roll into a row and bounce once more. **3.00** they dive → splash. | The strongest "how did they do that" moment, but the slowest category read: at 100 ms it can look like an abstract ad (ad-gist / mystery risk). **Not chosen.** The before/after spine (that was the launch ad's idea, and the owner banned reusing it); a face-first owner moment
(the only faces are AI clips, which turn the AI label on, see §7); a talking head (no footage; TikTok itself advises against static talking heads).
A no-face hook carries an *unquantified, probably modest* handicap. The "+27% CTR with human presence" figure traces only to an
unofficial mirror of a Meta PDF [W]. We offset it with motion onset, surprise, sound, big type and an owned character, and we test a
labelled face arm later (§6.5).

### 3.4 How to be "legendary" in 20 s (brand-science principles applied)
1. **One idea, one feeling, one CTA.** If a second idea creeps in (a services list, a price, process steps), cut it [M].
2. **The brand mark is the story.** The ••• is a *character with agency*: it types, dies, revives, works, multiplies and replies. System1 calls this a
   **fluent device**, and recurring characters are among the most effective assets ([System1](https://system1group.com/blog/the-seven-habits-of-highly-effective-characters) [M]).
   Owned assets beat borrowed ones ([Ipsos, 2,015 ads](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/2022-03/power-of-you-ipsos.pdf) [M]). Treat this film as **episode 1**: every future TASH ad reuses the dots, the sonic logo and the reply.
3. **Surprise at the open, rising joy in bursts, peak = brand, end on a high.** Surprise concentrates attention, and changes in joy retain viewers
   (Teixeira 2012 [S]). Judgements follow the peak and the end (Baumgartner, Sujan & Padgett 1997; [Fredrickson & Kahneman 1993](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.65.1.45) [S]).
   Put the brand *in* the climax, not after the story resolves (Neuro-Insight "conceptual closure", [Silberstein et al. 2020](https://frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fnins.2020.00292/full) [M]).
4. **Spectacle that shows the product truth.** Volvo "Epic Split" and Old Spice made B2B and FMCG brands famous with *one continuous, impossible
   move* [M]. Our version is the one-take where the dots become lights and the product builds itself. That is visible effort (the labour illusion,
   [Buell & Norton 2011](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/mnsc.1110.1436) [S, lab]) without a left-brain process montage.
5. **A human insight, said gently.** Owners see their product as "just a product nobody talks about". Show the gap, never mock it (Dove lesson [M]).
6. **Talkability is the goal and the mechanism.** The tagline *is* the brief. The payoff should be something owners would forward to a friend who has a project.
7. **Kuwaiti specificity carries the distinctiveness.** Dialect («دزّيته للقروب»), the Khaleeji score (claps, mirwas, Hijaz), chat culture.
   Don't rely on the near-black + lime palette alone (CRAFT flags that palette as a generic "AI-slop" pattern).

### 3.6 The three dots: why they appear more than "once or twice"
BRIEF §1 says to use the device "once or twice per piece, not sprinkled". Here the dots are not decoration. They are the *protagonist*,
the role INVENTORY gives them («our own mark and its three dots can carry that role»). They play hook → turn → crew → payoff → peak → reply → loop.
**Rule:** the dots never appear as a separator, bullet or wipe in this film. They only *act*.

## 4. Craft rules

**Pacing.** The hook is one composition (no cuts 0–3.0). The body averages 0.75–1.5 s per beat, *except* the one-take hero (3.5 s, slowest and richest).
Shot lengths shorten into the chat bloom, then linger on the sting and the CTA (CRAFT §2.2). Never run three equal-length beats in a row.
Cut only on reading boundaries: no cut while a line is being read. Fast pace plus high arousal overloads memory (Lang et al. 1999 [S]),
so the proposition, sting and CTA sit on held, simple frames with the music dipped. Use ≤10 unrelated scene changes per 30 s. Every join is
a *related* cut (dot → light, cap → ring, bubble → dots → ش).

**Type (Arabic RTL).** Alexandria 800–900 for hero lines at 120–170 px; IBM Plex Sans Arabic 500–600 for support lines at 44–60 px; JetBrains Mono for slates at 22–26 px.
Animate per word or per line, never per letter, with no letter-spacing and no kashida. Bidi: use U+200F (RLM) so «؟ ! …» sit on the left.
Hero lines are ≤3 words and ≤6 words on screen at once. **Hold time:** CRAFT §4.4 for ≤3 words; for lines of ≥4 words use the slower
**0.4 + 0.45 × words s**, because Arabic reads more slowly than English (no verified Arabic words-per-minute figure exists, so this errs safe).
**Never pace Arabic at TikTok's "5–10 words/s":** that is a text-reveal cadence and faster than adult *English* silent reading (≈4 words/s) [S].
Contrast ≥4.5:1 over moving frames. Lime only on ink, never lime text on paper.

**Sound (all original: `_system/audio/tashaudio.py`).** A crisp transient within 0.3 s (the typing ticks). The **typing tick is the sonic
motif**, heard at 0.0, 2.55, 9.75–12.75 and in the sting. One deliberate silence (2.0–2.3, H1). Three hero hits only: the splash (3.0),
the clap build (12.0), the snap (13.5). The score is Khaleeji-modern (group claps, mirwas, Hijaz pluck) with a hummable phrase, not just a beat.
Foley is frame-accurate (glass, spray, ring ting). Master at −14 LUFS / ≤−1.5 dBTP, with the energy in 150 Hz–5 kHz for phone speakers.
**Optional, worth testing:** the owner records two lines on his phone in native Kuwaiti («طشّ!» on the splash, «اطلب إعلانك بالخاص» on the CTA).
That is human presence without a face. Never use a synthetic voice: it reads as AI and on TikTok may need an AIGC label.

**Sound-off legibility.** Every idea in the film is also on screen in Arabic: the problem, the name, the proposition, the payoff, the CTA. If the
owner's voice-over is added, burn in matching text, because platform auto-captions handle Kuwaiti dialect badly. Test: a stranger watching
muted at 50% size can say who it is and what they do by 5 s.

**Loop.** The last 0.6 s puts the dots back in the frame-0 bubble position and colour, so a replay feels continuous. Never shorten the CTA hold to make the loop work.

**End card + CTA.** One CTA line, one handle, nothing else: no services, prices or guarantees (those go in the caption, the highlights and the DMs).
The card is fully readable for ≥3.5 s, keeps one thing alive, and ends in motion, not on a frozen card or a fade to black. The in-video CTA
must match the button: Instagram «اطلب إعلانك بالخاص» ↔ "Send message"; TikTok «دزّ لنا ••• بالخاص» ↔ profile and DM.
The CTA must never *look* tappable: no drawn send button, reply box or arrow-button.

**Finish and QC.** Kit grain ≤0.06 and one grade. Run CRAFT §7 hard gates, then the scorecard (ship ≥80/100, no line below 2). Check a contact sheet at 2 fps,
check every Arabic string twice, and preview with the safe-zone overlays on (§5).

## 6. Boost plan

### 6.1 Which route

  Messaging apps → Instagram only** (Messenger and WhatsApp OFF) → **maximise messaging conversations**.

  **Advantage+ creative enhancements OFF**, item by item: music, text variations, touch-ups, aspect-ratio crops ([Meta: some are on by default](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/297506218282224) [M]).
- **Instagram in-app Boost** only as a fallback (budget < ~KWD 5/day, or Ads Manager not set up): goal **More messages → Instagram chat**, never "profile visits".
  It has no A/B test tool, less control and, probably, no 3-s/ThruPlay columns [W].
- **TikTok:** post organically on @tash.creative (original sound), then promote it as a **Spark Ad** in Ads Manager. Objective, in order of preference:
  (1) Lead generation → **Conversations / TikTok direct messages** *if it appears for a Kuwait account* (help pages say this goal is limited to six SEA markets [W, dated]);
  (2) **Community interaction → Profile visits** (works anywhere; DMs are then counted by hand);
  (3) Promote → "More messages" if offered, else "More profile views". The DM inbox must be open to *Everyone*, with a welcome message set.
  Settle the open TikTok refund ticket noted in `HANDOFF.md` before any new Promote spend.
- **Not used:** WhatsApp destinations (owner: DM only); lead/instant forms (they need a privacy-policy URL, and TASH has no site); video-views or
  engagement objectives as the main goal (they find watchers, not owners).

### 6.2 Targeting
Kuwait (people living in), minimum age 18 (suggestion 21–55), languages Arabic + English, **Advantage+ audience, no interest stacking**
([Meta treats detailed targeting as a suggestion](https://www.jonloomer.com/detailed-targeting-suggestion/) [M]; TikTok: "fairly broad" beats narrow [M]).
The **creative does the targeting** («مشروعك» / categories in the first second).
**No separate women-owner ad set** unless each ad set alone can fund ≈7 × the cost per conversation per day. Until then H2w runs as one ad *inside* the single ad set.
Check the gender delivery breakdown on day 3 and split only if women owners are clearly under-delivered *and* the budget allows.

### 6.3 Budget and test structure
No credible Kuwait CPM or cost-per-conversation (CPCS) benchmark exists. The only figures are an unsourced KWD 1.5–4 CPM and EUR 1.5–8 per
click-to-WhatsApp conversation [W]. **Plan by formula:**
- Exiting learning needs ≈50 optimisation events in 7 days, so **daily budget ≈ 7.14 × CPCS** (Meta's rule of thumb ≥10×) ([Meta](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/942374239243867) [S]).
  At a CPCS of KWD 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 4 that is ≈ KWD 3.6 / 7.1 / 14.3 / 28.6 per day. A daily budget can overspend by up to 75% on some days, so quote the owner the **weekly** figure.
- TikTok: ad group ≥ USD 20/day (≈ KWD 6.2), campaign ≥ USD 50 if a campaign budget is set ([TikTok](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/budget?lang=en) [M]).
- Break-even CPCS ≈ close rate × first order. With an assumed 5% close × KWD 40–90 → **≈ KWD 2–4.5**. Replace the assumption with the real close rate after 20 conversations.

**Budget tiers (B = owner's daily budget, KWD; the owner must state B):**
| B | Setup |
|---|---|
| < 5 | IG in-app Boost of H1 (More messages → Instagram chat) for 7 days. No formal hook test; post H2/H3 organically (Trial Reels if available) and read skip rate as a directional signal only. No TikTok spend. |
| 5–15 | **Week 1:** Meta **A/B test**, 3 arms (H1/H2/H3 × 20 s), same objective, equal budgets, 7 days. **Week 2+:** one consolidated ad set with the winner at 20 s + 12 s + H2w. Add TikTok (1 ad group, ≥ KWD 6.2/day) only if B ≥ ~12. |
| ≥ 15 | As above, plus TikTok from week 2. A second ad set (e.g. women owners) only if each ad set ≥ 7 × CPCS/day. |

**Choosing the optimisation event after calibration:** (a) B ≥ 7 × CPCS and CPCS ÷ qualified share ≤ break-even → conversations. (b) 2–7 × CPCS →
conversations in one ad set, accepting "Learning limited" (a stability warning, not a stop). (c) < 2 × CPCS, or CPCS above break-even →
switch to ThruPlay/Reach and capture DMs organically (caption, pinned comment, end card). Fix the *hook* before adding budget.

**Hook-test rule.** Ads inside one ad set are *not* randomised: delivery favours early leaders ("divergent delivery", Braun & Schwartz, *J. Marketing*;
Braun, de Langhe, Puntoni & Schwartz 2024, *JCR* [S, recalled]). So: Meta **A/B test tool** for the screen, read on **hook rate × hold** (≈1,250 impressions/arm detects 25% vs 30%; ≈3,400 detects 25% vs 28%).
Read conversations per arm as directional only: Meta wants ≥100 events per arm before calling a winner ([Meta A/B](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/1738164643098669) [S]).
No mid-test edits. Launch every variant together and change things in weekly batches (each creative change restarts learning [S]).

### 6.4 Metrics and thresholds (working bars until we have our own baseline)
| Metric | Definition | Working bar / action |
|---|---|---|
| Hook rate (Meta) | 3-s video plays ÷ impressions | Replace the hook if <20% after ≥3,000 impressions; ≥30% is good (practitioner figures, cold B2B audience [W]) |
| Hold (Meta, 20 s) | ThruPlays (=15 s) ÷ 3-s plays; plus the 25/50/75/95/100% quartiles | Compare arms. If the 20 s hold is weak, promote the 12 s cut. The quartile drop shows which beat to fix. |
| Hold (Meta, 12 s) | ThruPlay ÷ 3-s plays = completion (Meta counts ≈97% of a sub-15 s video) | — |
| Hook (TikTok) | 2-s views ÷ impressions; early hold = 6-s ÷ 2-s views; average play time ÷ 20 s | **Never compare with Meta's 3-s rate** (different thresholds) |
| CPCS | spend ÷ messaging conversations started (≥7 days of inactivity counts as new) | ≤ break-even (≈ KWD 2–4.5 to start) |
| Qualified share | by hand: business owner with a project or a budget question ÷ all DMs | If <40%, strengthen the owner call-out in the hook and the CTA |
| Cost per qualified conversation | CPCS ÷ qualified share | the number we actually manage to |
| DM → quote → order | manual log (date, platform, source tap or ••• keyword, business type, qualified, quoted, ordered, value) | true close rate after 20 conversations |
| Frequency (7-day) | impressions ÷ reach | see refresh rule |

Never judge by "views" (they include replays) or by blog benchmarks. Every number above is a working bar, not a promise.

### 6.5 Creative refresh and the later test arms
- **Refresh rule** (Kuwait is small, so frequency climbs fast): swap in a new hook batch when 7-day frequency >2.5–3 **and** CPCS rises >25% for 3+ days,
  or when hook rate falls >20% (relative) from week 1 [W, heuristic]. Keep the body, sting and CTA constant; rotate only 0–3 s.
- **Hook bank for refreshes** (same body): «ما مسكتهم أول ثانية؟ راحوا.» · «منتجك يستاهل سالفة.» · H1 played in the women-coded form «مشروعچ حلو…».
- **Test arm "proof"** (week 3+, only if B allows a clean A/B): the same film with 0.8–1.2 s of STAKK/HABBA photoreal and/or MOR, slated and
  **AI-labelled**, against the code-only master. It tests whether real-looking food and café proof beats the label's trust cost. No public data shows the label's effect on delivery [W].
- **Test arm "face/voice"**: the owner's recorded voice line vs music-only, or a 0.5 s `s5_owner` beat (AI → labelled).

## 8. Kuwait specifics

**Dialect.** Natural Kuwaiti: «شلون، شنو، وايد، الحين، دزّ، يبيله، عندچ». No Saudi, Egyptian or Levantine forms («أبغى، إيش، كدا، هلّأ، واجد، يمديك»),
no MSA voice-over, no stacked slang, no Arabizi. Any voice is a native Kuwaiti speaker; the owner himself is ideal. Public copy uses the generic masculine; H2w uses «عندچ».

**Calendar (Umm al-Qura, computed; Kuwait moon sighting can shift by ±1 day [S]):** 11.11 = Wed 11 Nov 2026 · Black Friday / «الجمعة البيضاء» = Fri 27 Nov
· Isra & Mi'raj ≈ Tue 5 Jan 2027 (holiday) · **1 Ramadan ≈ Mon 8 Feb 2027** · Gergean ≈ 22 Feb · **National Day Thu 25 Feb = 18 Ramadan** ·
Liberation Day 26 Feb · Eid al-Fitr ≈ Tue 9 Mar. The biggest selling window for food, coffee, perfume and fashion in 2027 is one dense block in Feb–Mar,
and its ads must be commissioned in **Dec–Jan**.

**Flights (decision).**
- Produce Mon 9 – Sat 14 Nov. Post organically **Sun 15 Nov, 20:30** (Kuwaiti week starts Sunday; usage peaks at about 20:00–01:00) on both platforms.
- **Flight 1 (Mon 16 – Sat 28 Nov):** A/B screen days 1–7, consolidated ad set from Mon 23 Nov. **No pause over Black Friday.** Pausing restarts learning, and the
  "BFCM crowds Kuwaiti auctions" claim is weak [W]. If CPM rises >50% *and* CPCS sits above break-even for 3 days, cut the budget once by ≤30%.
- **Flight 2 (≈ Tue 1 Dec – ≈ Wed 20 Jan):** a new batch with the evergreen film plus a Ramadan-angle CTA card variant: «رمضان قريب… وإعلانك يبيله وقت.» → «اطلب إعلانك بالخاص».
  It is true, with no date and no deadline. The hero film never carries a date.
- **Pause rule:** if national mourning is declared or a major crisis hits, pause all paid and playful posting immediately (precedent: 40 days of mourning in Dec 2023 [M, recalled]).

**Culture.** Modest dress; no Quran, adhan or religious phrases as jokes; no royals, flag gimmicks, sect, tribe, citizenship or politics; no exploitation of boycott sentiment.
A quiet "استوديو كويتي" identity is fine. Word of mouth (السالفة، الديوانية، القروب) is native here, so the tagline is culturally owned, not imported.

**Regulation (lawyer to confirm; none of this blocks a November launch):**
- **Decree-Law 102/2026 (media):** reported published 4 Oct 2026, in force ≈ April 2027, with an MOI licence for social-media advertisers
  ([Lexis ME](https://www.lexismiddleeast.com/news/2026-10-05_21/en), [GLA](https://www.glaco.com/blog/kuwaits-new-media-law-one-framework-for-print-broadcast-digital-media-and-influencers/), [WEFAQ](https://www.wefaqlaw.com/en/news/kuwait-issues-new-media-regulation-law-with-licences-for-websites-influencers-and-advertisers) [M]).
  **It is not in force in Nov 2026.** Whether a company promoting itself on its own account needs the licence is undetermined; the wording leans "yes". **Milestone: legal answer before ≈ March 2027.**
- **Decree-Law 10/2026 (digital commerce):** ads must show the provider's name, price, description and contact details, *unless* they run from a registered account whose
  profile shows them ([GLA](https://www.glaco.com/blog/kuwaits-new-digital-commerce-law-what-businesses-need-to-know/) [M]). **So the profile carries:** the registered trade name (AR/EN), the MOCI
  licence/registration number once issued, contact (DM), a one-line service description, and a price basis (the approved «يبدأ من ٤٠ د.ك» reel price in the «الأسعار» highlight).
  The ad carries only the handle and the CTA, with **no licence number in the creative**.
- **The real launch gate:** the owner confirms TASH holds a valid MOCI commercial or home-business licence whose activity covers advertising and production. No discounts,
  giveaways or "first N free" without a MOCI permit [W, recalled].
