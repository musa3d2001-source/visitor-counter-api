## 1. The idea

**عربي.**
الشين في «مشروعك» فوقها ثلاث نقاط: نقاط طشّ، ونفس نقاط «يكتب الحين…».
المشروع اللي محد يسولف عنه تطيح نقاطه، والكلمة تصير «مسروعك»: نفس الشكل، بس ما تنقرى.
طشّ يرجّع النقاط. النقاط تصير ثلاث لمبات تحدّد المشروع بالنور، وأول ما ترجع تقعد على الشين يشتغل النور كله، والكلمة تنقرى من جديد.
والسالفة تبدأ: «من وين طلبتيه؟!»
والكلام اللي يصير عن مشروعك… هو طشّ. نفس النقاط، بدون ما تتحرك من مكانها، تصير «طشّ»، وبآخر الإعلان ترجع لمشروعك.

**English.**
The ش in «مشروعك» ("your project") carries three dots. They are TASH's dots, and they are also the "typing…" indicator. When nobody
talks about your project, the talk breaks, the dots fall off, and the word stops reading: «مسروعك» is the same shape saying nothing.

TASH brings the dots back. They become three studio lamps that outline your project in light. The moment they sit back on its ش, the full
light comes on and the word reads again. Then the talk starts: «من وين طلبتيه؟!» ("Where did you order it from?!").

The talk turns out to be **طشّ**. The logo forms around dots that never left their place. At the end the dots are handed back to your
project, typing, beside a single line: «اطلب إعلانك بالخاص» ("order your ad by DM").

**Why only TASH can own it.**
- No other mark is both a typing indicator and the dots of a letter in the viewer's own word for their business.
- Only Arabic lets a word stop reading when its dots go.
- It follows MOR's method (the brand mark is the story; the film goes out and back through the same shape) using TASH's own mark. It shares
  nothing with the launch ad. There is no product or person before/after: the only change is the viewer's own word, from dark to lit.
  There are no chat bubbles, no AI people, no wire→clay→final, no ring match cut and no reply bubble.

## 3. Audience and job

**Who.** Kuwaiti owners of small and medium Instagram/DM businesses: food and cafés, perfume and oud, abayas and fashion, beauty
(«مشغل», lashes), retail, home businesses. Mostly nationals, men and women. ~95% are not buying an ad today.

**What they believe.** «منتجي زين» (my product is good), but the post goes quiet and nobody brings it up in the قروب or the ديوانية.
They think «الإعلان الزين يبيله وكالة» (a good ad needs an agency budget).

**The single job.** Tie one feeling («يسولفون عنك», being talked about) to one name (**طشّ**) and one asset (**•••**, plus the tick
sound). Then leave one action: a DM.
- For the 95%: remember طشّ and the dots when they need an ad.
- For the 5%: «اطلب إعلانك بالخاص», which matches IG's "Send message" button, with the «يبدأ من ٤٠ د.ك» price in caption line 2.

**Proposition:** «خلّ إعلانك علينا» (leave your ad to us). **Feeling sold:** quiet pride. A service list is not sold.

## 4. The master: 20.000 s, frame-accurate

**Constants (`11-boost-ad/src/cues.json`, read by the 2D page, the 3D rig and `audio.py`):**
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, 600 frames. 120 BPM, so 1 beat = 15 f. Bar 1 (the drop) = 3.00 s (f90). Downbeats at 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13, 15, 17, 19 s.
- **Anchor A = (600, 490)** = the centre of the dot row at every hero moment and in the loop. With the hero word at origin x 124 and
  baseline 596, Alexandria's native ش cluster centre sits at (600.4, 487.3), so the row replaces it in place.
- **Dots:** Ø28 / pitch 33 on «مشروعك». Ø33.5 / pitch 39.4 on the wordmark at s = 480/2579 (the brand unit D = 180 units).
- **Typing:** `typing(t + 0.25, i, {period: 1.0, phase: 1/6, amp: 24})` from `kit.js`, squash ≤8%. Dot *i* peaks at t ≡ i/6 (mod 1),
  so the ticks fall on f0 / f5 / f10 of every second: one right→left triplet per second, which is also the Khaleeji clap figure.
  Amp is 11 on the wordmark and 16 during the calm CTA hold.
- **Hero word:** «مشروعك» is drawn as its **dotless body run** (س forms; `uniFD2A` for the شر ligature) from `word.json`, as one SVG path.
  Alexandria 900, 140 px, Paper, origin x 124, baseline 596. Body ink x 131–785, y 470–638.
- **Contact shadows:** each dot casts a soft Ink ellipse on the letter tops beneath it (y ≈521–530). It is 1.1×Ø wide and 8 px tall, multiplied
  and masked by the word path, with blur 3 px. Opacity is .30 at rest and .08 at the 24 px apex.
- **Text slots:**
  - L2 (hook lines): baseline 775, Alexandria 800, 84 px.
  - Talk slot (above the word): baseline 400, Alexandria 800, 84 px.
  - L3 (proposition / tagline): baseline 815.
  - CTA: baseline 755, Alexandria 700, 64 px.
  - Handle: baseline 840, JetBrains Mono 600, 40 px.
- **3D studio:** a warm-grey paper sweep (floor, cove, wall) with a soft backdrop pool behind the word. QC on every 3D frame:
  - ≥30% of pixels above sRGB 48;
  - nothing below sRGB 14;
  - ≤ sRGB 40 above y 430, where the talk line sits.
- **Finish:** grain .04, ±1.5-level dither noise in every gradient, vignette −12%, one grade.
- **Blur grammar = shutter:** sub-frame accumulation (N 4–8) on fast **2D** dot moves only. There is no accumulation in 3D, because nothing
  there moves fast. Blur-resolve on words is a focus effect.

## 9. Sound and score plan

**Sources.**
- `11-boost-ad/src/audio.py` builds on `/home/user/visitor-counter-api/tash/_system/audio/tashaudio.py` (pure synthesis, no samples).
- It adds stems recorded tonight from `src/assets/rec/`.
- It reads `src/cues.json` and places every SFX relative to its shot start.
- The style is Khaleeji-modern at **120 BPM in D Hijaz**. Bar 1 = 3.00.

**One motif.** The typing tick, pitched **an octave down** from the old ticks to D6 / E♭6 / F♯6 (1174.7 / 1244.5 / 1480.0 Hz). It has a
woodblock body (a `membrane()` layer with high damping) under the transient, so it is less shrill and less like a phone notification. It
plays as a right→left triplet 1/6 s apart. The same three notes are the hummable phrase, and in the crowd the triplet becomes the clap.
The oud is dropped: there is no Karplus–Strong "oud" and no Hijaz stab.

**Recording session (tonight, ≈30 min, phone in a quiet room, ≈50 cm away, airplane mode, to our 120 BPM click):**
- **Claps.** The owner and 3–4 friends.
  - 4 passes × 16 bars on 2 & 4.
  - 4 passes × 16 bars of the triplet: three quick claps starting on beats 1 and 3.
- **Voice 1.** A man: «مشروعك حلو…», warm, like telling a friend. 3 takes.
- **Voice 2.** A woman: «من وين طلبتيه؟!», delighted and curious. 3 takes.

  every line. Remux when the stems arrive (audio-only re-render).

  swung shaker. −6 dB reading dip under the proposition.
