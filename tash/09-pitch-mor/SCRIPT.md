## الفكرة (عربي)

**«الباب مفتوح… تفضّل.»**

شعار مور هو قفل باب، «سيدة» من أبواب الكويت الخشبية القديمة. فالإعلان كلّه يمرّ من هالقفل. نبدأ قدّام باب خشب
عنابي، ومن فتحة القفل نشوف زاوية مور بالليل والاسم منوّر. المفتاح يلفّ والقفل ينفتح، والكاميرا تدخل من الفتحة
لين نصير برّا قدّام المحل، وتبدأ الموسيقى. «فيه باب بالجهراء…»، «…وراه قهوة»: صبّة V60 على البار النحاسي. «تسوى المشوار.»: كوب
مور العنابي والثلج. بعدها مونتاج دافي على الإيقاع: إسبريسو على الصينية الحمرا، والمشروب الوردي من فوق، وقطعة البيكان.
وآخر شي المراية والجربيرة الحمرا، والموسيقى تهدى: «الباب مفتوح…» وبعد نفَس قصير «تفضّل.». هنا الكاميرا ترجع وتطلع من نفس
القفل، والقفل يصير شعار مور: الوردة تتفتّح، والاسم «مور MOR» ينكتب من اليمين لليسار. آخر شي توقيع طشّ ولوحة «إعلان
تجريبي غير رسمي».

## The idea (English)

MOR's emblem is a keyhole, the key plate of an old Kuwaiti wooden door. The film goes **through** it and comes back **out**
of it. It opens on a burgundy lacquered door. Cut into it is MOR's keyhole, keyed from the logo plate, with its own cream
outline, and through the hole the MOR corner glows at night. The key turns (0.42) and the lock gives (0.62): the door
knocks and the light inside comes up. We push through the keyhole, and the music starts on bar 1 as the door clears the
frame (1.6). There is no cut: it is the same m1 shot all the way. Then «فيه باب بالجهراء…» / «…وراه قهوة» / «تسوى المشوار.» run over
the corner, the V60 and the burgundy cup. A warm montage on the beat follows, then the mirror. «الباب مفتوح…», a
breath, «تفضّل.». The camera backs out through the same keyhole onto MOR's own burgundy plate, the emblem's rosette and
plant bloom inside it, and «مور MOR» writes on right→left. The film ends on the Kuwaiti welcome, «تفضّل», and the door that
opened the film is MOR's logo.

**What it buys MOR:** a brand film where the logo *is* the story, not a sign-off. The film works with sound off, the
line stays true without any claim, and the first frame and the end card rhyme (keyhole → keyhole) so the reel loops.

## Grade and finish (`src/grade.js`, one per-pixel pass, a pure function of t)

- A per-clip match (`main.js → MATCH`: black point, exposure, white balance, saturation) brings seven clips to one warm night.
- The shared look: a soft highlight shoulder, blacks lifted to a burgundy-black (20, 9, 11), and whites capped at MOR cream (250, 241, 224), so nothing clips to pure white.
  A constant vignette, kit `grain` at .045 overlay, and a base scale ≥ 1.03 on every shot.
- Graduated falloffs (per shot, per format, in `layout.js`) seat each line of type in shadow. They are not boxes or scrims.
- Palette: burgundy / crimson / cream / brass / wood from the footage and the plate. **No TASH lime before the tail.**

## Known weaknesses (honest)

1. **The MOR sign's "O" is an AI artefact.** In m1 the O folds like a heart around the corner of the sign box (MOR's real sign has a plain O). The edit only uses m1's *wide* part, as the CD notes advise, and after the CD review the **poster no longer shows the sign**. It still shows, small, in the hook (0–1.4 s) and S1. The CD offered a 1.2–1.5× punch-in that would keep the sign out of frame, but it wasn't done: the sign is the subject the keyhole frames in the hook, and a punch-in would remove the point of the shot. Only regenerating the footage can fix it
2. **The cups' small printed Arabic is softened, not fixed.** The AI misprinted it: in m3 the top-right block is garbage, and in m7 it reads «الكميت» for «الكويت». `tools/track_patch.py` tracks each block frame by frame (NCC ≥ 0.92), and the page softens it past legibility with a feathered box blur in both formats and on the poster. It reads as focus falloff on the curved cup, but a frame-by-frame look shows a soft patch where print should be. The English "Kuwait / Al jahra city" and «مور MOR» stay sharp. On the poster, the cup's «MOR مور» wraps around the cylinder, so its outer letters fall off the curve (that is the footage itself)
3. **The hook's first 12 frames barely move** (5 % creep). The first event is the key turn at 0.42 s and the knock and light-up at 0.62 s. A stronger event in 0–0.4 s would sharpen the hook.
4. **«…وراه قهوة» sits on the brass bar beside the white scale.** m2 has no clean dark area wide enough for a centred line, so it is seated with a graduated falloff and a soft shadow. It is legible, but it isn't on a clean field.
5. **The low end is still bass-heavy.** About −7 dB of the mix sits at 20–60 Hz. That is mostly the bass line's own roots (G1, A1, B♭1), which phones play through their harmonics. The mix is within spec.
6. **The door in the hook is procedural** (a lacquered-burgundy grain texture plus a light falloff), not real wood footage. It reads as a dark lacquered door, not as a carved Kuwaiti door.
7. **The end card → TASH tail joins with a 0.2 s dip to ink** (not a designed transition). It is clean (no overlap) but plain.
8. Small slate type (22 px mono, 30 px Arabic) is legible and within the brief's label sizes, but it is small.
