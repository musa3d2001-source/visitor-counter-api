# MOR «مور» spec pitch ad: CD brief

**What this is:** the owner of TASH asked for a strong, real-looking video ad for **MOR «مور»**, a real specialty café in
Al Jahra, Kuwait (Instagram `@morkwt`), to show what TASH can do. MOR has **not** commissioned this. It is a **spec pitch**:
made to be shown to MOR (for example in a DM), not posted as client work. So:
- The film ends with a clear slate: «إعلان تجريبي غير رسمي — مقترح من طشّ لـ مور» + `SPEC AD — NOT COMMISSIONED BY MOR`.
- No invented facts: no prices, hours, menu claims, awards, reviews or "best in Kuwait". Only what the photos show.
- MOR's mark (keyhole emblem + «مور MOR») is used only to pitch to MOR. `footage/ai/frames/logo_plate.png` is a
  faithful recreation for this pitch only. Do not alter the design.

## MOR's identity (read from the owner's reference photos)
- **Name:** «مور» in a flowing calligraphic Arabic with a long baseline stroke, plus **MOR** in classic high-contrast serif capitals, set side by side.
- **Emblem:** a keyhole. A round floral rosette sits on an ornamental keyhole body with a tulip/plant motif. This echoes the
  keyholes of old Kuwaiti wooden doors. **It's the brand's big idea.**
- **Colours:** deep burgundy (cups, about `#5E1F28` to `#6B2430`), crimson-red trays (about `#A3232E`), cream (`#F3E7D3`), warm
  oak/walnut wood, brushed brass/gold (a slatted bar drip-grill).
- **Objects:** burgundy double-wall cups ("Kuwait · Al jahra city" / «الكويت · مدينة الجهراء»), white espresso cups with a burgundy band
  on saucers with many concentric burgundy rings (retro), red trays embossed with MOR and the keyhole, origin cards
  with flag stripes, wax paper printed with the logo, and a resin-and-wood slab board.
- **Space:**
  - Outside: a white smooth-plaster corner building under a residential tower, a backlit **MOR** sign in a dark wood box,
    warm-lit wooden French doors, pink bougainvillea and uplit young trees.
  - Inside: warm wood shelves with LED glow, ceramics, a round
    wooden vanity mirror on a brass stand, red gerberas, a cactus in a pink pot, cream cushions. It feels homely, vintage-warm and intimate.
- **Drinks and food:** V60 pour-over, iced filter coffee, espresso, a pink-red frothy signature drink, a pecan bar.

## The idea: «الباب مفتوح… تفضّل.» ("the door is open… come in")
MOR's own emblem is a keyhole. The film starts *through* it and ends with the door open, the Kuwaiti welcome «تفضّل».

Suggested structure (9:16, about 20–22 s, warm Khaleeji lo-fi at about 90 BPM). Improve it if you have something stronger:

| Time | Shot | Text (Kuwaiti) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.6 | **HOOK**: black. The MOR keyhole emblem appears as a cut-out of light; through it we see the glowing MOR corner at night. Key-turn click. Push *through* the keyhole into the shot | — |
| 1.6–4.0 | `m1_exterior`: the night corner, sign glowing, leaves moving | «فيه باب بالجهراء…» |
| 4.0–6.0 | `m2_pour`: gloved hands, V60 spiral pour on the brass bar | «…وراه قهوة» |
| 6.0–7.6 | `m3_iced`: the burgundy cup hero, ice and foam (logo legible) | «تسوى المشوار.» |
| 7.6–12.2 | Quick, warm montage on the beat: `m4_espresso`, `m5_red` (top-down), `m6_pecan` | — (or tiny mono labels, no claims) |
| 12.2–15.0 | `m7_mirror`: slow dolly past the mirror, gerbera reflection | «الباب مفتوح…» → «تفضّل.» |
| 15.0–18.0 | **MOR end card** from `logo_plate.png`: the keyhole emblem + «مور MOR» on burgundy, revealed like a door opening | `@morkwt` · «الكويت · الجهراء» |
| 18.0–20.5 | **TASH tail**: short signature (createSting or the short sting) + the spec slate | the slate above |

Type: Arabic per word or line, RTL, never split letters, and never over busy detail. MOR's world is burgundy, cream and brass, so the type should
sit in MOR's palette and not TASH lime (lime appears only in the TASH tail). Use the existing OFL fonts (Alexandria / IBM Plex Sans Arabic).

Sound: original, using `_system/audio` (tashaudio). Use night ambience (soft crickets, a distant car) under the hook, a key turn and a wooden door,
a kettle pour, ice clinks, a porcelain clink, fizz, warm oud-ish plucks, soft claps and a round bass, then MOR's end card on a warm resolve and the TASH sonic logo.
Mix to −14 LUFS.
