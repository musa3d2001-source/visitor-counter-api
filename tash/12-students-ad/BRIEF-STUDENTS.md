# «طشّ للطلاب» — students ad: CD brief

**What this is (owner decision, 25 Nov):** TASH opens a second line beside the ad studio: digital work for **school and
university students** in Kuwait — presentations, reports, research papers, posters, project videos, and anything in
Microsoft Office. The owner's words: «نبي هالاعلان لحسابنا طش بخليه مو بس استديو لترويج المنتجات بل حتى للطلاب الجامعة
وغيرهم اسوي لهم مشاريع و عروض وكل شي ببالهم رقمي يبونه او microsoft office».

This is an ad for **TASH's own account** (not a spec for another brand). The line's label is **«طشّ للطلاب»**.

- **Format:** 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps, **25–30 s** (target 28). For TikTok, Reels and Snap.
- **Tone:** fast, youthful, confident, Kuwaiti dialect, talks to one student directly.
- **Owner asked for:** cinematic SFX, a young confident Kuwaiti voice-over, and **real sample work on screen** rather than
  marketing talk. He gave full creative freedom («لك مطلق الحرية في الاختيار والابداع والتفكير»).
- **Owner's working rule:** act as a real ad director. Make the dynamic, appealing choice yourself. Never ask about craft.

His suggested beats: 0–4 confused student at a laptop among papers · 4–8 fast cinematic transition + brand · 8–18 samples
of designs, projects, videos, presentations · 18–24 quality and details · 24–30 logo + contact + «اطلب الآن».

It is too long and too formal for 28 s and reads like a brochure. Rewrite it in TASH's voice

## Hard rules for the copy (non-negotiable)
1. **We design, format and produce. We do not write the student's graded work for them.** The line is:
   **«المعلومة منك… والترتيب والتصميم علينا.»** Never «نكتب بحثك»، «نحل واجبك»، «نسوي مشروعك عنك»، «بحوث جاهزة».
   Research and reports are sold as **تنسيق · ترتيب · تدقيق شكلي · تصميم** (layout, structure, formatting, cover, charts,
   references formatting). This is honest, and it protects the TASH name with schools and universities.
2. **No promises we can't keep:** no grades («الدرجة الكاملة», A+), no "overnight / خلال ساعة" delivery, no "دكاترة ومختصين",
   no client counts, no "الأفضل". The hook dramatises a deadline panic, so the ad must **not** imply we rescue you the same
   night. A light honest twist is welcome, e.g. «بس لا تخلّيها لآخر ليلة».
3. **Samples are samples.** Everything shown is real design work made by TASH for this ad, on fictional topics, labelled
   small «نماذج من تصميمنا». Never "من أعمال عملائنا". No real school/university names or logos, no real people.
4. **No Microsoft logos or exact Office UI.** A generic presentation/doc app look is fine. File-type chips
   (`.pptx .docx .xlsx .pdf .mp4`) and the words «برزنتيشن / بوربوينت / وورد / إكسل» in copy are fine.
5. **Contact is DM only:** «راسلنا بالخاص». Instagram **@tash.creative.kw**, TikTok **@tash.creative**. No phone, no
   WhatsApp, no Snapchat handle (we don't have one).
6. Arabic: Kuwaiti, natural grammar («على الإنستقرام», never «بالإنستقرام»). RTL, never broken letters. Arabic-Indic digits
   in Arabic lines. Proofread every rendered string by eye.

## The idea: «السلايد الفاضي»
Every student knows the blank slide at 2 a.m. The film starts there and **طشّ** (the splash) turns it into work you are
proud to show: «شغل يبيّض الوجه». Their own closing line «خلّ شغلك يتكلم عنك» becomes TASH's tagline turned to the student:
**«خلّ شغلك يسولف عنك.»** (TASH tagline: «طشّ… خلّهم يسولفون عنك!»).

## Look
- TASH brand for the ad's own graphics: ink `#0B0B0B`, lime `#C6FF00`, paper `#F5F5E9`; Alexandria (display), IBM Plex Sans
  Arabic (text), Archivo (Latin display), JetBrains Mono (labels). Tokens: `tash/_system/tokens.css`. Logo: `tash/02-logo/`.
- The **samples** use their own palettes and type moods to show range (they are "client-style" work, not TASH-branded).
- Motion: fast, snappy, on the beat; real depth (CSS 3D / perspective, shadows, parallax); every transition motivated.
  Safe zones for 9:16 social: keep text inside x 64–1016, y 250–1620.

## Sound
Original only (`tash/_system/audio/tashaudio.py`). About 124 BPM, bouncy and bright (UK-garage / Jersey-club feel, not
dark). Hook: keyboard taps, a notification ding, a clock tick, low tension. Splash: a big «طشّ» hit (water splash +
sub + riser cut). Parade: one percussive hit per card, paper flips, mouse clicks, whooshes. Payoff: wide chord. End: TASH
sonic logo. Mix −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. When the VO is present, duck music about 6–8 dB under it.
