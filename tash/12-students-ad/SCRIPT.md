# «طشّ للطلاب» — the launch ad for TASH's student line · «المرة الجاية… خلّ التصميم على طشّ.»

This is an ad for **TASH's own account** that opens the new line «طشّ للطلاب»: design, layout and production of
students' digital work (presentations, reports, research formatting, posters, videos, CVs, any Office file). It is not
a spec for another brand. **Status: built, not delivered to the owner, not approved, not published.**

> **Nobody has listened to the voice-over.** Claude cannot hear audio. The dialect, the pronunciation («باچر»،
> «الياية»، «بريزنتيشن»، «طشّ»، «سي في»), the cuts inside the read, the 1.08× tempo-up and the moved breath were all
> judged from measurements only. **The owner judges the dialect** before anything is posted. The music and the sound
> effects have not been heard by anyone either.

الإعلان يبدأ من ورا طالب حاط إيدينه على راسه قدام لابتوب شاشته بيضا، والأسئلة تنزل مع الإيقاع: «عندك برزنتيشن؟ تقرير؟ بحث؟».

## The idea (English)

**"The blank slide."** Every student knows the blank slide at 2 a.m. The film opens behind a student with both hands in
his hair in front of a laptop with a white screen; the questions punch in on the beat: «عندك برزنتيشن؟ تقرير؟ بحث؟».
The camera dives into the screen to white and we are inside a blank generic slide app: 2:47 a.m., a group chat «منو
مسوّي البرزنتيشن؟؟», a reminder «التسليم: باجر ٨:٠٠ ص», and the one big line «والتسليم… باجر؟».

A message from TASH lands over the reminder and types itself: «المرة الجاية…» / «خلّ التصميم على •••». The dots swell on
the riser and **burst into a lime splash that floods the whole screen** and washes the student's file away (it is
never shown finished: the ad does not promise a same-night rescue). The dots fall into the wordmark «طشّ.», «للطلاب»
snaps under it, and TASH's first sample surfaces out of the lime.

The samples then answer the three questions in the same order, each dealt onto one lit spot on a beat: presentation
«يشدّ من أول سلايد.», report «نرتّبه.», research «ننسّقه عدل.» (headings, numbering, references), poster, video, CV and
an Excel dashboard «وأي ملف أوفيس.», all labelled «نماذج من تصميمنا». Details: a tilted contents page whose rows and
guides snap onto the margin on «نضبّطها.»; then the rule line word for word on one before/after slide: «المعلومة منك…
والترتيب والتصميم علينا.» (we design and format; we do not write the student's work). The same slide goes up on a
lecture-hall screen (match cut): «ويوم التسليم… خلّ شغلك يسولف عنك.» End card: the logo, «للطلاب», «شغل يبيّض الوجه.»,
«اطلب الحين — دزّ لنا بالخاص», both handles, and the twist «بس مو آخر ليلة.», which loops back into the hook.

## The VO edit

- Source: `vo/take1.mp3` (ElevenLabs `eleven_v4`, voice «Jassim Almathkour» `lB6K69u17Gb2FSiT9pVV`, generation
  `78njtU9gFZpS7XmjjyqV`, 28.32 s). Take 2 (`G1C1EOc1VgQpsji2ZXsx`, 28.88 s) is not used; the owner was asked which
  take he prefers and has not answered.
- The read was cut into 24 phrases at measured silences (`src/tools/vo_pieces.py`; the aligner was up to 0.36 s off,
  so the cut points come from the waveform, not the word timings), sped up 1.08× with rubberband (pitch and formants
  kept) and placed on the 125 BPM grid (`src/tools/vo_edit.py`): every key word starts within 8 ms of its beat or
  half-beat. The take's one inhale was moved from before «حتى الهوامش» to before the rule line (film 19.21–19.58 s).
  Result: `src/vo/vo_edit.wav` (voice 0.10–27.96 s) + `src/vo/vo_timeline.json` (phrase and word times).
- In the mix the music is ducked 6.3–7 dB under the voice (median 7.0 dB); the voice is de-essed and levelled per
  phrase. The splash lands on the spoken «طشّ» by design (voice over background only 4.5 LU on that phrase).

## Sound

Original, synthesised by `src/audio.py` with the house engine `_system/audio/tashaudio.py` (no samples, no stock, no
paid tool): 125 BPM in F major, UK-garage 2-step with swung hats and Jersey-club kick rolls into the turns; the home
chord (F) is held back to the payoff chord and the sonic logo. The sonic logo is rebuilt from the TASH ingredients and
timed to the cues (ticks f834, hit f848) instead of the 4.2 s `tash-sonic-logo.wav`, whose ticks would fall under
«آخر ليلة». Both mixes are mastered to −14 LUFS, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, then muxed with a two-pass linear loudnorm.
Every SFX is placed on its cue frame (all 39 placed events within 1 frame of `cues.json → sfx`, checked from the
audio's own event log; the clean hits measured in the stems sit within 0.2 frame).
