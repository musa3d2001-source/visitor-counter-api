## 1. Display name

| | Name | Length |
|---|---|---|
| Current | «طشّ \| صناعة إعلانات 🎬» | 21 / 30 |
| **Recommended** | **«طشّ TASH \| استوديو إعلانات»** | 26 / 30 |
| Fallback (keeps the current wording) | «طشّ \| صناعة إعلانات» | 19 / 30 |

**Why change it**
- **Keep the keyword «إعلانات».** The name field is the only searchable text besides the handle, and that's the word business owners type.
- **Use «استوديو», not «صناعة».** «استوديو» says *we produce*; «صناعة إعلانات» sounds like the name of an industry, not a place. It also heads off "تديرون حسابي؟" messages before anyone sends them.
- **Add «TASH» in Latin.** It matches the stacked lockup (طشّ over TASH) and catches people who search in English.
- **Drop 🎬.** Every video account uses the clapperboard, and the old bio used it too. Our mark is the three dots, not a film icon.
- **Keep the shadda in طشّ.** It's in the logo. *Unsure:* whether Instagram search matches «طش» typed without the shadda. After the change, search «طش» from another account. If we don't come up, drop the shadda in the name field only.
- Instagram allows two name changes every 14 days. Decide once.

## 2. Critique of the current bio

<div dir="rtl">

> 🎬 نصنع إعلانات تخليهم يسولفون عنك!
>
> ⚡ فيديوهات إعلانية | ريلز | تيك توك
>
> 🇰🇼 صناعة إعلانية بإبداع كويتي
>
> 📩 اطلب إعلانك بالخاص

</div>

**What works:** line 1 is on-brand and sounds Kuwaiti («يسولفون»). Line 4 is a clear call to action («بالخاص»).

**What doesn't:**
1. **It looks like a template.** Four lines, each starting with an emoji, is the default agency bio. It reads like a checklist, not a voice.
2. **One word does all the work.** «إعلان» appears four times in four lines (إعلانات / إعلانية / إعلانية / إعلانك), and «نصنع / صناعة» twice more. Line 3 repeats line 1 and the display name.
3. **«بإبداع كويتي» says nothing.** Nobody can check it, everyone uses it, and «إبداع» is the most worn-out word in Arabic agency copy. Show the creativity; don't claim it.
4. **Three of our five services are missing.** It lists video and two platforms, but not design, ideas & scripts, or motion & editing. Ideas & scripts are what set us apart.
5. **The scope is missing.** Nothing says *we make the ad itself*, so we'll get "manage my account" and "how much for followers" messages.
6. **The punctuation is noisy.** Pipes plus emoji plus «!» on a phone screen. ⚡ adds nothing.

## 3. Bio options (≤150 characters, measured)

All three cover only our five services, use at most two emoji, and promise nothing we can't control.
Each paragraph below is one bio line. Paste them as consecutive lines with no empty lines between. The counts include the line breaks.

### A: «الواضح» (Clear). 135 characters

<div dir="rtl">

نسوي الإعلان نفسه: من الفكرة لين الملف النهائي.

فيديو · ريلز وتيك توك وستوري · تصميم · أفكار وسكربت · موشن ومونتاج

📩 اطلب إعلانك بالخاص

Says exactly what we do and where it stops. It's safe, but it has the least personality.

### B: «النقاط» (The dots). 138 characters. **Recommended**

<div dir="rtl">

خلّهم يسولفون عنك •••

نسوي الإعلان نفسه: فكرة وسكربت، فيديو، تصميم، موشن ومونتاج — للريلز والتيك توك والستوري.

🇰🇼 الخاص مفتوح — اطلب إعلانك بالخاص ↓

</div>

### C: «الجريء» (Cheeky). 126 characters

<div dir="rtl">

هذا منتجك. وهذا إعلانه.

فيديو، ريلز وتيك توك وستوري، تصميم، أفكار وسكربت، موشن ومونتاج.

إحنا نسويه… وإنت تنزّله.

📩 الخاص مفتوح

</div>

It rhymes with the launch-ad hook. «إحنا نسويه… وإنت تنزّله» states the boundary as a wink: we make it, you post it.

### Why B
- **Line 1 is the whole brand idea in one short line.** The tagline plus «•••» is the typing indicator, and it rhymes with the lime dots in the avatar right next to it. No other bio can say that.
- **Line 2 is honest and complete.** «الإعلان نفسه» quietly rules out account management, and all five services are named, with social ads given as their platforms.
- **Line 3 does the job of three lines.** The flag gives location without a sentence, «الخاص مفتوح» is warmer than «اطلب بالخاص», and «↓» points at the link below (TikTok). Contact is DM only for now.
- **Two emoji only**, and neither starts a line as a bullet.
- Switch to **C** later if the launch ad becomes the thing people recognise us by.

## 6. Category

Instagram's category list changes. Search the picker and pick in this order:

1. **The closest *production* category** it offers (search "production" or "video"), for example a video-production or production-company type. That says *we make things*.
2. **"Advertising/Marketing"** if no production category fits. It's broad but searchable.

**Avoid:** "Advertising Agency" and "Marketing Agency" (they suggest campaign and account management, which we don't do), and "Digital Creator" (reads as an influencer).
Show the category label on the profile. It's a free line of explanation under the name.
