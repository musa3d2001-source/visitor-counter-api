# Pinned posts

## Recommendation: pin 9, 3, 8, in that order

| Slot | Post | Job | What the visitor thinks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **9 · Launch ad** | Who we are, in one reel | «هذول منو؟» → «استوديو إعلانات.» |
| **2** | **3 · LAYL perfume** | Proof of the ceiling | «أبي نفس هالمستوى حق مشروعي.» |
| **3** | **8 · شلون تطلب إعلانك؟** | The next step | «…شلون أطلب؟» |

The brief's target reaction is «أبي نفس هالمستوى حق مشروعي. شلون أطلب؟». These three pins say it in that order: **see → want → act**.

### Why this set
- **9 (launch)** is non-negotiable. It's the only post that explains the whole studio, and it's the one most new visitors arrive from.
- **3 (perfume) over 2 (burger) and 1 (coffee).** The pinned proof should show our *highest* level, not our average. LAYL is the most crafted piece (3D glass and liquid, lighting, slow-motion mist), and premium work tells the client lower-budget work is safe with us. The reverse doesn't hold.
  *Counter-argument:* food and coffee businesses look like a big share of the Kuwaiti small businesses that advertise on Instagram (an assumption, not data), and the burger is the most relatable to them. That's why slot 2 should **rotate** (below), not stay on the perfume forever.
- **8 (how to order) over 7 (services) or 4 (إحنا طشّ).** The bio and the «خدماتنا» highlight already list the services, and the bio's «الإعلان نفسه» covers the scope. What a convinced visitor still lacks is *how*, and 8 answers it in one swipe.

### Why the order changes from the original proposal (9, 8, 3 → 9, 3, 8)
The proposal puts the ask before the proof. Reading order on the pinned row (left to right in English, right to left in Arabic) should be **trailer → proof → ask**. Asking someone to order before they've seen the best work is a cold ask.

## How to pin
- Instagram usually shows the **most recently pinned** post first. Pin in reverse: **8, then 3, then 9.** Check the profile, and unpin and re-pin if the order comes out wrong.
- Pinned tiles show the **3:4 grid crop**. Check that the cover title of 9, 3 and 8 sits in the centre (see `07-ops/07-deliverables-and-formats.md`, reel cover).
- If the 9 launch tiles were designed as one continuous 3×3 mosaic, pinning will break it. Decide which matters more. For a studio account, pins win.

## When to change the pins
- **Slot 2 rotates** to the strongest new piece each time a new concept ad goes up, or to the concept closest to the sectors that actually DM us (judged from real conversations, not a number we publish).
- **The first real client ad (with written permission, per `07-ops/09-delivery-message.md`) takes slot 2.** Real work beats concept work.
- **Slot 1 stays the launch ad** until there's a stronger "who we are" piece, such as a showreel that includes real client work.
- **Slot 3 stays «شلون تطلب؟»** permanently, unless the process changes and a new version replaces it.
