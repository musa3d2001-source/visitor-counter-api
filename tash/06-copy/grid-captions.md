## Rules used in every caption
- **Each paragraph is one caption line.** Blank lines between them are fine on Instagram; tighten them if you prefer.
- **Line 1 is the hook.** It has to work alone, because Instagram cuts the caption after about 125 characters.
- **Concept ads say so by line 2**, so the label shows before «المزيد»: «إعلان تجريبي لعلامة خيالية — ونقدر نسوي مثله لمشروعك.» The video carries the visible `CONCEPT AD` slate as well.
- **One CTA per caption**, closed with our «•••» (the typing dots). Never more than once per caption.
- **Hashtags: up to 8, and the first five are the core set.** Instagram has been capping hashtags (there are reports of a five-per-post limit). If the app refuses eight, keep the first five.
- **Arabic hashtags drop the hamza on purpose** (`#اعلانات_الكويت`) because that's how people type them. Body copy keeps correct spelling (إعلان).
- **Never hashtag the fictional brand names** (حبّة / STAKK / LAYL). They could collide with real businesses.
- **Alt text is under 100 characters, in Arabic.** If the app doesn't offer custom alt text on Reels, skip it there. The burned-in text and caption already describe the ad.
- Visual details marked **(تحقق)** are based on the brief, not the final cut. Check them against the render before posting.
