# Content calendar: the first 4 weeks after launch

## Assumptions
- **The launch date isn't fixed**, so weeks are relative: Day 0 is the day the launch ad (post 9) goes up.
- **Kuwait's working week is Sunday to Thursday.** Feed posts go out **Sunday / Tuesday / Thursday**: 2–3 a week, with the third slot optional in weeks 3–4.
- **Posting time:** start around **8–9 pm Kuwait time**. That's a starting guess, not data. After two weeks, use the account's own Insights (most active times) and move.
- **Only formats we can make now:** 2D motion reels, 3D product reels, carousels and static designs, behind-the-scenes screen captures, and stories with stickers.
- **Not yet:** testimonials, case studies or results (no clients yet), on-location shoots (not offered, per `07-ops/01` `/shoot`), talking-head videos (only if the owner wants to be on camera), and trending audio (we use our own original music, per `07-ops/08` §5).
- **For production:** save WIP captures from every project (storyboards, wireframe/clay/render passes, timeline and audio screen-recordings). The «ورا الكواليس» series depends on them.

Series rules:
- Every business in «فكرة إعلان» and «قبل/بعد» is **generic or fictional**: «محل ورد»، «مغسلة سيارات». Never a real name. Label it: «فكرة لمشروع خيالي» or «منتج تجريبي لعلامة خيالية».
- «قولها بالكويتي» uses invented stiff lines, never a real brand's copy. Start from the rewrites in `voice-and-tone.md` §3.
- A new concept ad takes the longest. Start production in week 1 so it can go out in week 4.

## Weekly review: Thursday, 20 minutes, private

- **Look at:** saves, shares, profile visits, link taps, DMs that mention a specific post, and which sectors people DM from.
- **Decide:** next week's «فكرة إعلان» sectors, which concept sits in pinned slot 2, and what to drop.
- **Never** publish these numbers or turn them into claims ("our reels reach…"). They're for us to decide with, not to brag with.

## Guardrails (all four weeks)
- Concept or fictional work is labelled on screen **and** in the caption, every time.
- No real brands, logos, people or restaurant names. Generic phone and chat UI only.
- Our own audio only. No trending sounds.
- No fake DM screenshots, no "what clients say" posts, no follower giveaways.
- Client work appears only with written permission (`07-ops/09-delivery-message.md` §4). The first one replaces a concept in the pins.
